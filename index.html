The Problem

Your tools track tasks.
None of them guard your scope.

Jira. ClickUp. Trello. Great for tracking tasks. But clients and teammates add new work through Slack and email — and your timeline quietly breaks.

The Slack Change

"Hey can we just add one more thing?" — A casual message that costs you 3 days. No record. No approval. No accountability.

📧

The Email Assumption

How ScopeLock Works

Three steps to scope certainty.

Designed for freelancers, small teams, and agencies. No onboarding call. Start in under 5 minutes.

01

🔒 Lock the Scope Baseline

Write what's included in the project — and explicitly what's NOT. Once locked, it becomes a permanent record. Every stakeholder knows exactly what was agreed, and when.

02

📋 Log Every Change Request

When a client wants to add, remove, or change something, you submit a formal Change Request with description, estimated hours, and additional charge. Nothing gets done from a Slack message ever again.

03

✅ Approve or Reject — With Full Audit Trail

You approve or reject each change — with an optional rejection reason visible to the client. Every decision is timestamped and recorded forever in the audit log.

04

📊 Watch Your Scope Drift Dashboard

At any moment, see how much work has deviated from the original scope. Generate an invoice from approved changes in one click — with actual hours and charges, not guesses.
